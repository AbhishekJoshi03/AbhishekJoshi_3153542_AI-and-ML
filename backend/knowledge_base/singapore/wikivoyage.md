## Destination overview
Singapore is a city-state in Southeast Asia with a mix of modern urban development, Asian cultural influences, food, shopping, parks and nightlife.

## Neighbourhoods and districts
Useful areas for travellers include:
- Singapore CBD and Marina Bay
- Chinatown
- Little India
- Bugis and Kampong Glam
- Orchard Road
- Sentosa and Harbourfront
- East Coast

These areas provide different experiences, including heritage and culture, shopping, food, museums, waterfront attractions, beaches and entertainment.

## Transportation
Singapore's public transportation network includes MRT and LRT trains and buses. The MRT/LRT network covers many important visitor areas and is described as fast, reliable and affordable.

Taxis and rideshare services are also available. Singapore is pedestrian-friendly, although heat and humidity can make long walks tiring.

## Culture and food
Chinatown, Little India and Geylang Serai provide opportunities to experience different cultural communities. Singapore is also known for hawker food and diverse Asian cuisines.

## Weather and practical advice
Singapore has a tropical climate with high temperatures and humidity. Rain can occur throughout the year, often as short heavy showers. Travellers should consider carrying an umbrella and staying hydrated.
