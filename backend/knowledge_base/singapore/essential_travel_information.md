# Essential Singapore Travel Information

Source TITLE: Essential Singapore Travel Information – Visit Singapore
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/essential-travel-information/
Source type: Official tourism resource

## Weather and timezone
Singapore has a tropical climate. Visit Singapore describes typical temperatures around 25–33°C and recommends light, airy clothing and carrying an umbrella for occasional rainfall. Singapore Standard Time is GMT+8.

## Language
English is widely used. Mandarin Chinese, Malay and Tamil are also spoken. Visitors may encounter Singlish, a colloquial form of English used in Singapore.

## Connectivity
Wireless@SGx provides free Wi-Fi access at participating hotspots for visitors using foreign mobile numbers through the Wireless@SGx application.

## Useful services
Singapore Visitor Centres can provide visitor assistance, itinerary planning, attraction information and other tourism support.

## Money and payments
The official currency is the Singapore dollar. Visit Singapore notes that credit cards and contactless payment methods are widely accepted.

## Public dining etiquette
At hawker centres, food courts and coffeeshops, visitors should return trays and crockery to designated return stations.

## Travel planning
Travellers should check current entry requirements and advisories before travelling because requirements can change.
