# Things To Do in Singapore

Source TITLE: Things To Do & Must-Visit Places in Singapore – Visit Singapore
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/things-to-do/top-things-to-do/
Source type: Official tourism resource

## Attraction categories
Singapore offers activities and attractions covering culture, heritage, nature, entertainment, shopping, food and family experiences.

## Marina Bay
Visit Singapore highlights Marina Bay as a modern district with attractions including:
- Merlion Park
- Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay
- Gardens by the Bay
- Singapore Flyer
- Marina Barrage
- Marina Bay Sands
- ArtScience Museum

The district combines waterfront views, architecture, cultural venues, nature attractions and family-friendly options.

## Chinatown
Chinatown offers heritage, temples, food and cultural experiences. Examples include:
- Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum
- Masjid Jamae
- Sri Mariamman Temple
- Thian Hock Keng Temple
- Chinatown Heritage Centre
- Chinatown Street Market
- Maxwell Food Centre and Chinatown Complex

## Family and indoor activities
Singapore has indoor and family-oriented activities that can be useful alternatives during hot or wet weather. Examples across Visit Singapore material include interactive museums, indoor attractions and family experiences.

## Food and local experiences
Singapore's travel experiences include hawker centres, local food, heritage neighbourhoods and cultural districts.

## Itinerary support
Visit Singapore provides itinerary material for different trip lengths and traveller profiles, including family, food, cultural and multi-day travel planning.
